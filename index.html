03 / METHODOLOGY

Dual-Discipline Execution: From Concept to Operational Launch

Exceptional hospitality and residential environments require a balance between creative direction and rigorous operational execution. Our studio bridges interior decoration with site management—transforming existing architectural volumes into fully operational assets. From initial concept and FF&E procurement to final staging, every phase is engineered to protect asset value, meet strict operational standards, and honor spatial identity.

Phase Milestone Operational Scope & Strategic Deliverables
01 Strategic Audit & Asset Brief Comprehensive evaluation of spatial distribution, target demographic alignment, and investment parameters. We establish the functional baseline and aesthetic strategy without structural alterations.
02 Concept Design & Spatial Curation Development of a tailored interior identity balancing durability, hospitality standards, and visual refinement.
Furniture & Custom Joinery Architectural Lighting Material & Textile Palette FF&E Specification
03 Procurement & Financial Management Rigorous selection and budgeting across commercial suppliers. We evaluate lead times, material longevity, replacement guarantees, and supply chain reliability to keep production on budget and on schedule.
04 Site Direction & Logistics Control Direct oversight of vendor deliveries, trade schedules, and on-site assembly. We ensure complete alignment between the approved design scheme and technical execution.
05 Staging & Operational Turnkey Final styling, equipment placement, and white-glove quality audits. Spaces are handed over in full operational readiness for immediate guest check-in or occupancy.
04 / ABOUT ME

Interior design with an operational mindset.

I'm Jorge Ali, a decorator and project professional specialised in B2B interior design and hospitality environments. My work focuses on transforming existing spaces through interior design, decoration, furniture, lighting and FF&E selection, without altering the architectural layout of the property. Working directly on hospitality projects such as Aparthotel Fuenlabrada Central has shaped the way I approach design: not only considering how a space looks, but also how it will operate once it opens. I coordinate the process from the initial concept through supplier sourcing, procurement, installation and final setup, ensuring that every decision remains aligned with the project's budget, positioning, durability and day-to-day operational needs. CELTADeco is therefore positioned between design and execution: creating functional, coherent interiors and helping owners and operators turn an existing asset into a space that is ready to operate. Based between Barcelona and Madrid, working primarily on hospitality, serviced apartments, coliving and real-estate projects.
