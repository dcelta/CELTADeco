02 / APPROACH

Dual-Discipline Execution From concept to opening.

Great spaces require both meticulous creative intent and absolute operational precision. Our methodology balances architectural preservation with high-end corporate utility, ensuring that every space is both visually refined and built to perform. From structural remediation to final interior styling, every asset is carefully developed to deliver hospitality-grade reliability, premium remote-work integration, and seamless maintenance upkeep, without losing its local character or architectural identity. CELTADeco provides a practical B2B interior design process focused on taking an existing space from concept to operational readiness.

Phase Focus Area Scope & Key Operations
01 Brief & Asset Understanding Every project starts by understanding the asset, its target guest or user, positioning, operational requirements, and investment framework. The existing architectural distribution becomes the starting point for the interior design strategy.
02 Interior Concept & FF&E A visual and functional concept is developed around the existing space, selected for aesthetics, durability, maintenance, and operational use:
  • Furniture & Lighting
  • Materials, Finishes & Textiles
  • Decoration & Equipment
  • Guest-room & Common-area Requirements
03 Budget & Procurement Translates the concept into a realistic purchasing plan. Suppliers and products are evaluated according to design, cost, availability, lead times, durability, replacement possibilities, and operational suitability to protect the concept while keeping it executable.
04 Supplier & Installation Coordination Coordinates suppliers, deliveries, and furniture installation on site as purchasing begins. The focus is on maintaining complete consistency between the approved concept, budget, and final execution.
05 Setup & Opening Covers final setup and property preparation for opening, reviewing spaces from both a design and operational perspective. Furniture placement, equipment, decoration, and final details are verified so the asset transitions smoothly into day-to-day operation.
03 / ABOUT ME

Interior design with an operational mindset.

I'm Jorge Ali, a decorator and project professional specialised in B2B interior design and hospitality environments. My work focuses on transforming existing spaces through interior design, decoration, furniture, lighting and FF&E selection, without altering the architectural layout of the property. Working directly on hospitality projects such as Aparthotel Fuenlabrada Central has shaped the way I approach design: not only considering how a space looks, but also how it will operate once it opens. I coordinate the process from the initial concept through supplier sourcing, procurement, installation and final setup, ensuring that every decision remains aligned with the project's budget, positioning, durability and day-to-day operational needs. CELTADeco is therefore positioned between design and execution: creating functional, coherent interiors and helping owners and operators turn an existing asset into a space that is ready to operate. Based between Barcelona and Madrid, working primarily on hospitality, serviced apartments, coliving and real-estate projects.
